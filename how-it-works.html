How BimCar Connect works

Find a rental, understand the rules and keep the conversation with the company in one place.

1 · Find your car

Search around your plans

Choose pickup and return dates, a vehicle type and a pickup area. Compare the listed daily rates and use the filters to narrow down driver requirements, deposits and payment preferences.

Explore rentals →
2 · Check the details

Read the car’s requirements

Requirements can differ from one vehicle to another. Review age limits, licence experience, documents, payment terms, insurance, extras and the company’s cancellation policy before requesting.

About requirements →
3 · Ask and confirm

Send a booking request

Add a message with your request. The rental company checks the details and confirms the booking. Use the side Messages inbox to agree pickup arrangements and ask follow-up questions.

Open your trips →

The request comes first.

A booking request is not an instant confirmation. Wait for the rental company’s confirmation and read its rental agreement before making your travel plans around the car.

You pay the company directly using its accepted payment methods. BimCar Connect helps you compare and communicate; the rental agreement is between you and the rental company.

Booking questions →

Pick up with a plan.

Confirm the collection point, time, required documents and any deposit with the company. Let it know about changes to your arrival through Messages.

Check the vehicle together at collection and agree the return arrangements. For questions about a specific car or rental agreement, contact that company.

Run a rental company?

Keep your fleet and renter conversations together.

Create a company account

Create and complete your profile

Register, verify your email and add your company details. The platform team reviews company information before approving publishable listings.

Add vehicles and policies

Use general company requirements as defaults, then override them for individual cars. Add features, prices, pickup options and vehicle photos.

Keep availability current

Block offline bookings and maintenance, review requests, and reply to renters. Optional paid boosts are available; payment confirmation is required before activation.
