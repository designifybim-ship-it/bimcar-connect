Good places to stop. Better when you don’t rush. Match a west-coast lunch, an east-coast view or an Oistins evening to your island plans.

Food & bar stopsReviewed 6 November 2026

Looking for the best bar or food stop for your day? Start with the area you’re exploring and the kind of outing you want. These five editorial picks give you a mix of seaside pubs, beach dining and local food.

Driving today?

Make it a food-and-soft-drink stop. If you plan to drink alcohol, arrange a taxi or a designated non-drinking driver before heading out.

Bathsheba · St. Joseph

Round House

Ocean-view restaurant and bar

A natural choice to look into when your day leads east. Make Bathsheba the destination, spend time enjoying the village, then settle in for a meal with an Atlantic outlook. It fits a slower outing better than a hurried island circuit.

Venue / visitor information 
Speightstown · St. Peter

Fisherman’s Pub

Seaside pub and Barbadian food

Combine a walk around Speightstown with a lunch stop by the water. This is a good town-and-food pairing when you want variety without spending the whole day moving between destinations.

Venue / visitor information 
Batts Rock · St. James

La Cabane

Beachside restaurant and bar

A beachside option to consider before or after a west-coast outing. Plan it as a proper stop for food, conversation and the setting, then check the venue’s current booking and visiting details.

Venue / visitor information 
Mullins Beach · St. Peter

Sea Shed

West-coast beach dining

Make Mullins a relaxed part of a northbound day, or keep the outing centred on the beach and lunch. Check the current venue details before travel and leave enough time to enjoy the stop.

Venue / visitor information 
Oistins · Christ Church

Oistins Bay Gardens

Local food vendors, not a single restaurant

Choose Oistins for a food-led outing and take time to look at the options among the vendors. For an evening out, decide on transport before you arrive so the journey home is already handled.

Venue / visitor information 

Keep the plan easy.

Opening arrangements, menus, events and booking requirements can change. Use each linked venue or tourism page to check details before visiting. Choose one or two stops you can enjoy, rather than making an evening into a rush between venues.
