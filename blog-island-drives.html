Three island drives.
A different kind of day.

Choose a west-coast wander, a garden-to-Atlantic outing or a day that points north. Then make the pace your own.

Island drivesReviewed 6 November 2026

A good island drive needs a direction, a few stops and enough space to change your mind. These are suggested groupings, not navigation instructions or timed itineraries.

ISLAND DRIVE

West-coast wander

Holetown → Mullins → Speightstown

A flexible day of town stops, beach views and lunch. Start with a wander in Holetown, continue towards Mullins, and leave time to explore Speightstown on foot. You can shorten the day at any point rather than pushing on just to complete a circuit.

Make time for a stop. La Cabane at Batts Rock can be a stop before heading north; Sea Shed at Mullins or Fisherman’s Pub in Speightstown fit later in the day. Choose one meal stop and give it time.

ISLAND DRIVE

Gardens to the Atlantic

Hunte’s Gardens → Bathsheba

Make a garden visit the first part of your outing, then continue towards the east coast. Bathsheba gives the day a very different finish: Atlantic views, a walk and time by the village. Leave room for a late lunch instead of adding several distant attractions.

Make time for a stop. Round House is a possible meal stop in Bathsheba. Check its current arrangements and keep the plan flexible.

ISLAND DRIVE

The north is the destination

Speightstown → St. Nicholas Abbey / Cherry Tree Hill → North Point

Use Speightstown as an early town stop before heading into the northern countryside. Pair the estate and viewpoint if they suit your day, then make North Point the final destination. If your visit is becoming rushed, choose fewer stops and enjoy them properly.

Make time for a stop. Treat the cave visit as optional because sea conditions may affect access. If an estate visit includes a rum tasting, arrange a non-drinking driver or different transport.

Before you set off

  • Allow for your actual starting point, traffic, weather and daylight. No fixed drive times are promised here.
  • Check bookings, visitor arrangements and current access with the places you plan to visit.
  • Use current navigation, keep left and park away from the roadway before stopping for photographs.
  • Check your rental’s requirements, fuel policy and pickup/return arrangements.
  • If you plan to drink alcohol at a stop, use a taxi or a designated non-drinking driver.
