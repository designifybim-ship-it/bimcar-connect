Listing rules

Two rules every company agrees to when it lists on BimCar Connect.

Real photos only

Renters must see what they're renting

  • Photos must show the exact vehicle being rented.
  • Photos must be real and taken in the last 12 months.
  • No AI-generated, AI-edited, stock or manufacturer images.
  • Renters can report a photo. Listings that break this rule are taken down.
Listing site only

Your company is responsible for your rentals

  • BimCar Connect doesn't own, rent out, insure or inspect vehicles.
  • Your company handles licences, insurance, the Car Rental Levy and rental agreements.
  • Damage, accidents, breakdowns and disputes are between you and the renter.
  • BimCar Connect never collects or holds rental payments.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Listing your company and vehicles, posting specials, getting booking requests and seeing your stats are all free. Boosts are the only thing companies can pay for, and they're optional.

So how does BimCar Connect make money?

From optional boosts, and from tourism businesses like tours and restaurants that pay for clearly labelled sponsored spots. Renters never pay us, and we never take a cut of a rental.

Does BimCar Connect take a cut of my bookings?

No. Renters contact you and pay you directly.

How do renters pay me?

However you like. In your company profile, choose cash, card, bank transfer or your own online payment link. Set when renters pay, your deposit and your cancellation policy. Bank details and payment links are only shown after you confirm a booking.

How do I get the verified badge?

Upload your trading licence and insurance certificate in your company profile. We check them within two working days.

Who handles the Car Rental Levy?

You do, as with any rental. In your profile you can say whether your prices already include the BBD $5 a day levy, and we show that on your listings.

Can I remove my listing?

Yes, any time from your company profile. Your vehicles stop showing straight away.
