Getting started

Everything you need to set up your account, step by step. Steps marked Required must be done before you can book a car or list one.

FOR RENTERS

Set up your account and book a car

About 5 minutes to set up. Booking a car still takes a little back and forth with the company. You agree the details together in Messages.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Required

    Choose I'm renting on the sign-up page. Use an email you check, and a password of at least 12 characters.

    Create an account →
  2. 2

    Verify your email

    Required

    Open the email from BimCar Connect and tap Confirm my email. Check your spam folder if it isn't there within a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Add your details

    Required

    Go to My trips → Profile and add your full name, WhatsApp number, date of birth, and whether you're a resident or a visitor.

    Open my profile →
  4. 4

    Add your licence details

    Required

    Say where your licence was issued and the year you got it. Each car shows its own driver rules, such as minimum age and years licensed.

    Add licence details →
  5. 5

    Find a car and send a request

    Required

    Search by dates and pick-up place, compare prices and read each car's requirements. Then send a request. Your saved details fill in the form for you.

    Find a car →
  6. 6

    Agree the details in Messages

    Required

    The company replies in Messages, at the side of the site. Be ready to share a photo of your licence and agree the deposit and payment. A request is only booked once the company marks it Confirmed.

  7. 7

    Pick up your car

    Optional

    Bring your licence and the payment method you agreed. Check the car with the company at pick-up and take photos before you drive off.

FOR RENTAL COMPANIES

Set up your company and go live

Your dashboard shows a Required checklist until every step is done. Listing is free, with no commission.

  1. 1

    Create a company account

    Required

    Choose I run a rental company on the sign-up page. Add your company name, your name, email and WhatsApp number.

    List your company →
  2. 2

    Verify your email

    Required

    Tap the link in the email from BimCar Connect. You can then sign in to your dashboard.

  3. 3

    Set requirements and payment terms

    Required

    In Profile & requirements, set driver age, licence years, deposit, insurance, cancellation rules and how renters pay you.

    Set requirements →
  4. 4

    Complete your company profile

    Required

    Add a phone number, a short description and your pick-up places. Renters see this on your company page.

    Edit profile →
  5. 5

    Add your vehicles with real photos

    Required

    Add each car with its price per day, seats, transmission and fuel. Use your own recent photos of the actual car. Stock photos and AI-generated or AI-edited images aren't allowed.

    Add a vehicle →
  6. 6

    Switch your cars on and get approved

    Required

    Turn on the cars you want renters to see. BimCar Connect checks your details, usually within 1 business day. Your cars go live once you're approved.

  7. 7

    Answer requests and run your day

    Optional

    Reply to requests in Messages, then accept or decline them under Bookings. Use Today, Schedule and the pick-up and return checklists to run the business.

    Open the portal →
