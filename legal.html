Terms and privacy

How BimCar Connect works, what we're responsible for, and how we handle your information.

1. Who we are

BimCar Connect is an online listing service based in Barbados. Contact: bimcarconnect@yahoo.com.

2. What BimCar Connect is

BimCar Connect is a free listing site. It lets independent rental companies in Barbados list vehicles and lets renters compare them and send booking requests. BimCar Connect does not own, rent out or insure any vehicle and is not a party to any rental agreement. Each rental is a contract between the renter and the rental company. Renters pay the rental company directly, using the payment methods that company lists. BimCar Connect doesn't collect or hold rental payments.

3. Fees

Using BimCar Connect is free for renters, and listing is free for rental companies. We don't charge booking fees or commission. Rental companies can pay for optional boosts, and other businesses can pay for sponsored spots. Paid placements are always labelled "Featured" or "Sponsored". Prices are shown before you pay.

4. Rental companies

  • You must hold every licence, permit and insurance the law requires to rent vehicles in Barbados, keep valid insurance on every vehicle you list for its whole listing period, and show proof to BimCar Connect on request.
  • You are responsible for your prices, vehicles, rental terms, deposits, the Car Rental Levy and any taxes.
  • Your listings must be accurate and kept up to date, and photos must show the actual vehicle.
  • You are solely responsible for checking each renter's licence, age, identity and eligibility, for your rental agreement, and for the vehicle's safety and roadworthiness.
  • We may ask for documents and may suspend or remove listings or companies that are inaccurate, uninsured or break these terms.

5. Renters

  • Check the rental company's own terms, deposit, insurance and age requirements before you rent.
  • You need a valid driving licence and must follow Barbados road laws. You are responsible for how you and anyone you allow to drive use the vehicle, including any accident, damage, fine or unlawful act.
  • Disputes about a rental are between you and the rental company. We'll help where we reasonably can.

6. Reviews

Verified members can review BimCar Connect. A rental-company review requires a completed booking belonging to that renter. Reviews must be honest and relevant, and are checked before publication. We may decline or unpublish reviews that are fake, abusive or off topic.

7. Real photos only (listings and ads)

Rental companies must show real, recent photos (taken in the last 12 months) of the exact vehicle being rented. AI-generated, AI-edited, stock and manufacturer images are not allowed. Anyone can report a photo. We may remove any listing that breaks this rule, without notice.

The same rule applies to advertising. Ads must use real, unedited photos of the advertiser's actual business, food, venue or product. AI-generated or AI-edited images, and stock images presented as the real thing, are not accepted. We may remove an ad that breaks this rule without a refund.

8. BimCar Connect is a listing site only

BimCar Connect is not a rental company and is not responsible for any rental. We don't own, rent out, insure, inspect, maintain or deliver any vehicle. To the fullest extent the law allows, BimCar Connect is not responsible or liable for:

  • damage to or caused by any vehicle;
  • accidents, injuries or death;
  • loss or theft of a vehicle or personal belongings;
  • breakdowns, mechanical faults or the condition of a vehicle;
  • insurance, deposits, payments, refunds, fines or tolls;
  • any dispute between a renter and a rental company;
  • the accuracy of listings, prices or availability provided by rental companies;
  • the acts, omissions, crimes or misconduct of any renter, rental company, driver or other third party, on or off the platform.

Your rental agreement is between you and the rental company. Check their terms, insurance and the vehicle before you drive. Nothing here limits rights you have under Barbados law that can't be limited.

9. Messages and conduct

Messages are for arranging rentals. You must not send or post anything sexual, harassing, threatening, hateful, discriminatory, fraudulent or unlawful, or try to take payments or personal data dishonestly. Messages may be filtered automatically, and a conversation may be reviewed by BimCar Connect if someone reports it. We may block messages, suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, and share information with the Royal Barbados Police Force or other authorities where the law requires or allows.

10. Indemnity

Rental companies and renters agree to indemnify and hold harmless BimCar Connect, its owners and team against any claim, loss, damage, fine, cost or liability (including reasonable legal fees) arising from their vehicles, rentals, driving, conduct, listings, messages or breach of these terms.

11. Limit of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, BimCar Connect is not liable for any indirect or consequential loss, and our total liability to any user for any claim is limited to the fees that user paid BimCar Connect in the 12 months before the claim (for free accounts, this is zero). 12. Changes and governing law

Cookies and browser storage

BimCar Connect uses browser storage to keep you signed in and remember functional choices such as currency, saved cars, searches, message drafts and whether you have dismissed the guide or this notice. We don't use advertising trackers or third-party analytics. To count visits, the site keeps a random visit number in your browser storage. It isn't linked to your name, email or IP address, and visits are only ever shown as totals.

Fonts are served by BimCar Connect. Google Maps loads only when you select Load map; Google may then use its own cookies or storage. Opening external map, payment or WhatsApp links takes you to that service, where its privacy terms apply.

You can reopen this notice from the footer. You can clear site data in your browser settings; this will also sign you out and reset saved preferences on this device.

1. What we collect

  • Renters: name, email or WhatsApp number, and the details of booking requests you send.
  • Rental companies: company and contact details, vehicle listings and photos, and verification documents (trading licence and insurance).
  • Everyone: anonymous visit counts: which page was viewed, the website you came from (for example Google or Instagram), whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer, and whether your device is set to Barbados time. We don't record names, emails or IP addresses for this, and we only ever look at totals.

2. Why we use it

To run your account, pass booking requests to the rental company you choose, verify companies, show stats to companies, and answer your messages. We don't sell personal data.

3. Who we share it with

When you send a booking request, we share your name, contact details and request with that rental company only. We use GitHub Pages to host the website and Supabase to run accounts, listings, messages and account emails. They process data for us under contract.

4. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open and booking request records for 2 years after the rental date. Verification documents are deleted 90 days after a company leaves. Anonymous visit counts are deleted after 13 months.

5. Your rights

Under the Barbados Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email bimcarconnect@yahoo.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections and limit who can see personal data. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll notify the Data Protection Commissioner and affected people as the law requires.
