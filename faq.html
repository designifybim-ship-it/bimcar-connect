Frequently asked questions

The essentials about requests, requirements, messages, accounts and rental-company listings.

Is a booking request confirmed immediately?

No. A request is sent to the rental company, which checks the details and confirms the booking. Wait for its confirmation and review the rental agreement before treating the car as booked.

Why do requirements differ between cars?

Each rental company sets its policies, and an individual vehicle can have requirements that differ from the company’s general defaults. Read the requirements on the exact car you want, including age, licence experience and documents.

Can I search by my age or deposit preferences?

Yes. Use the vehicle filters to enter driver age and licence experience, and narrow down deposits, payment methods and other rental terms. Filters help you find matching policies; the company still checks your documents and confirms eligibility.

Why is a car greyed out?

A greyed car is marked unavailable for today or for your selected dates. You can view its availability or choose another vehicle. Availability depends on the company keeping its calendar current, and a request still needs confirmation.

Who receives my rental payment?

The rental company. Read its accepted payment methods, deposit and timing requirements, and confirm the full rental price directly with it. BimCar Connect does not collect the rental payment through the booking-request form.

Can I collect at the airport, cruise port or hotel?

Some companies offer those options. Check the pickup choices on the listing and confirm the exact meeting point, time and any delivery charge with the company. See the pickup-area guide.

Where do I read replies and ask questions?

Open Messages at the side of the site. Your booking conversations are grouped there, and your initial booking message appears in the thread. Sign in with the account used for the request to read and reply.

What if I need to change or cancel my rental?

Contact the rental company through the booking conversation and review its cancellation or change terms. Do not assume that sending a message automatically changes a confirmed rental or waives a charge.

How do I reset my password or verify my email?

Use the password-reset option on the sign-in page. For a new account, follow the verification email before signing in. Check your spam folder and contact the platform team if you cannot complete the process.

How does a rental company join?

Create a company account, verify the email and complete the company profile. Add your vehicles, requirements and availability. Platform review is required before approved listings can be published.

What does a paid boost do?

Boosts are optional paid placements for eligible listings. Read the available placement and duration details in the company dashboard. A payment request opens WhatsApp; a boost is not activated until the platform team verifies payment. A boost does not guarantee bookings.

How can I leave a review?

Open the reviews page and sign in with a verified account. You can review the website, or a rental company after a completed booking. Your display name appears publicly, and reviews are checked before publication.

Who should I contact about a problem?

For the car, pickup, payment, insurance or rental terms, contact the rental company. For account access or a problem using the platform, contact the BimCar Connect team.

Need a hand with something else?

Send the platform team a question, or message your rental company about an existing request.
