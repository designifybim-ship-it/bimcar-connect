Top 10 best places to drive in Barbados

A local-day shortlist for coastal scenery, countryside, historic towns and good reasons to pull over somewhere properly.

Places & viewpointsReviewed 6 November 2026

Our editorial top ten combines viewpoints, coastal stops, towns and visitor attractions. These are destinations to drive to: park responsibly, then enjoy each place on foot. The order is a shortlist, not an official ranking.

  1. 01
    St. Joseph · East coast

    Bathsheba

    For Atlantic views and a slower afternoon

    Make Bathsheba the centre of an east-coast day. The village, surf and offshore boulders give you plenty to take in without a packed itinerary. Park appropriately, then explore on foot and leave time for lunch.

    Treat the Atlantic shore as a sightseeing stop; don’t assume a scenic beach is suitable for swimming.
    Official visitor information 
  2. 02
    Northern countryside · St. Peter / St. Andrew border

    Cherry Tree Hill

    For a wide view across the island

    This is a good northern-country stop to pair with St. Nicholas Abbey. Let the viewpoint be a reason to pause rather than another box to tick on a rushed island circuit. The tourism listing places it in St. Peter and describes the surrounding property as bordering St. Andrew.

    Use a suitable stopping area. Keep bends and the roadway clear while taking in the view.
    Official visitor information 
  3. 03
    St. Lucy · North coast

    North Point & Animal Flower Cave

    For the island’s rugged northern edge

    Make the northern tip your main destination for a day with fewer stops. The cliff-top scenery is a draw in itself, and the cave can be an additional experience when access is available. Speightstown and the northern countryside make natural stops along the way.

    Cave access can depend on sea conditions. Check the operator’s current arrangements before setting out.
    Official visitor information 
  4. 04
    Castle Grant, St. Joseph · Inland

    Hunte’s Gardens

    For a green break from the coast

    Leave the car behind for a walk through the planted former sinkhole. A garden visit makes a different kind of island stop and works well as the first part of a day that continues towards Bathsheba. Build in time to enjoy the garden rather than racing through it.

    Check visitor arrangements before travel, and allow for the roads between inland stops.
    Official visitor information 
  5. 05
    Allen View, St. Thomas · Inland

    Harrison’s Cave

    For a day built around an underground experience

    Make the cave the anchor of an inland outing. Plan around your booked experience, then leave the rest of the day flexible for a meal or the surrounding countryside. This is a good choice when your trip needs more variety than another beach stop.

    Confirm the experience and arrival details with the operator; don’t build your day around an assumed walk-in slot.
    Official visitor information 
  6. 06
    St. Peter · Northern countryside

    St. Nicholas Abbey

    For heritage and an unhurried visit

    Pair the estate with nearby Cherry Tree Hill for a northern-country day. Give yourself time to explore rather than trying to fit every northern attraction into the same afternoon. Check the current visitor options before choosing your plan.

    If your visit includes a rum tasting, arrange a non-drinking driver or alternative transport before you go.
    Official visitor information 
  7. 07
    St. Philip · Southeast coast

    Bottom Bay

    For dramatic coastal scenery and a picnic stop

    Choose Bottom Bay for the scenery and time to slow down. Make it a planned stop rather than a quick detour, and leave the car in an appropriate place before exploring on foot. Bring what you need for a relaxed visit and take everything away with you.

    The official tourism listing advises against bathing because of the strong waves. Enjoy the view from a safe position.
    Official visitor information 
  8. 08
    St. Peter · West coast

    Speightstown

    For a seaside town and a good lunch

    Park, walk the historic streets and make the waterfront part of your visit. Speightstown is an easy place to give a west-coast day some variety: less time moving between stops, more time looking around. It also works as a pause before a longer northern outing.

    Choose proper parking and explore the town on foot rather than stopping in traffic for photos.
    Official visitor information 
  9. 09
    St. James · West coast

    Holetown

    For a west-coast wander

    Use Holetown as a town stop for shops, food and a change of pace. It can be the beginning of a northbound day or the main event when you want to keep plans simple. There is no need to add a long list of further destinations to make the outing worthwhile.

    Allow for traffic between west-coast stops and decide where to park before settling in.
    Official visitor information 
  10. 10
    Christ Church · South coast

    Oistins & Bay Gardens

    For a food-led outing

    Visit the fishing town and make the Bay Gardens food vendors part of the plan. Oistins is a place to spend time over a meal, rather than a stop to squeeze in at the end of an already full island drive. Check current arrangements with the venues you want to visit.

    For an evening involving alcohol, leave the rental parked and arrange a taxi or a non-drinking driver.
    Official visitor information 

Build a day, not a race.

Pick one area and two or three stops. Check opening arrangements, bookings and local road conditions before leaving; use current navigation rather than treating this list as turn-by-turn directions.
