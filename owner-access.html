BimCar Connect management

Platform sign-in

Use your designated management account to manage companies, listings, booking conversations, payments, and enquiries.

  1. Create your account with the designated management email assigned to BimCar Connect.
  2. Verify your email using the confirmation link. The designated management email receives admin access automatically after verification.
  3. Sign in and open the admin dashboard. Other accounts cannot grant themselves admin permissions.

Already registered? Sign in with your designated management email. If the verification email has not arrived, request another on the signup screen.
