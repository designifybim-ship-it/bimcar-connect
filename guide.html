Driving in Barbados

What you need to rent, the rules that catch visitors out, and three drives worth doing.

Rental levy. The Barbados Revenue Authority states that the Car Rental Levy replaced the Visitor Driving Permit on 15 October 2025. It is BBD $5 per day, capped at BBD $35 per rental, and included in the daily rental fee. Ask your company to confirm the full price and driver documents. Read the BRA guidance.

What to bring

A valid driving licence and a credit or debit card for the deposit. Some companies set a minimum age, often 21 or 25, so check the listing.

Keep left

Barbados drives on the left. Most cars are right-hand drive, so the gear stick and indicators may feel backwards for a day.

Roundabouts

The ABC Highway has a lot of them. Give way to traffic coming from your right and go clockwise.

Narrow roads, quick stops

Country roads are narrow and often unmarked. Route taxis and buses stop suddenly to pick people up, so leave space.

Fuel

Most stations have attendants who fill up for you. Many close in the evening outside the main towns, so fill up before heading east or north.

If something goes wrong

Call your rental company first. Police 211, ambulance 511, fire 311.
